This is a link to one creative coding artist I like.

Funnysandwich uses p5.js and code to generate illustrations.

One of her personal characteristics is that she often uses houses as an image in her work. The houses appear in different forms and different arrangements. The colors are usually bright and warm. The shapes of the houses are crooked and irregular, which makes the works look very cute.

I think the visual generated by coding is very interesting, because the same visual element can have many different variations. Although the houses appear again and again, every house can be a little different, so the image does not feel too simple or mechanical.

Creating with code also gives the artist more possibilities to explore. When changing some rules in the code, it can create very different visual results, and sometimes bring some unexpected surprises. Compared with drawing directly, code can help the artist try different possibilities more quickly. These unexpected results can also become part of the creative process.

Also, I really like drawing myself, and I am very interested in graphic and visual art. So I am easily attracted to her way of combining illustration with Creative Coding.
